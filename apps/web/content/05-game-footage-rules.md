# Game Footage Rules in Plain English: Copyright & Publisher Policies

When you record and post video game footage, you are working with intellectual property owned by the game's developer and publisher. 

While most major publishers welcome gameplay videos, streams, and clips, **publishers and platforms set their own rules**, and those rules come with clear boundaries. Violating them can result in video takedowns, channel copyright strikes, or account suspensions.

---

## 1. Copyright vs. "Fair Use": The Reality

Many creators mistakenly believe that adding a filter or playing a game automatically constitutes "fair use." In legal reality:
- Video game software, graphics, characters, code, voice acting, and storylines are protected works owned by publishers.
- Playing a game does not grant you copyright ownership over the game's underlying creative assets.
- Your right to create and post gameplay videos exists primarily through the **express video policies and licenses granted by game publishers**.

---

## 2. Universal Publisher Rules (What Is NEVER Allowed)

Across almost every major gaming company, the following actions will trigger immediate takedowns or legal action:

### A. Pre-Release Leaks & Unreleased Content
Publishers have zero tolerance for footage obtained through data breaches, hacked builds, leaked review copies, or broken street dates. Posting leaked footage will almost certainly result in immediate DMCA notices and potential channel termination.

### B. In-Game Radio & Commercial Music
Games often license real-world commercial music for in-game radio stations (such as in open-world racing or action titles). While the game studio paid for the right to play those songs inside the game, **that license does not transfer to your YouTube or TikTok channel**. The recording labels will automatically claim or mute your clips via Content ID. Always mute copyrighted radio stations in the game's audio settings.

### C. Extracting & Distributing Raw Assets
Isolating soundtrack files, cutscenes without gameplay, voice actor lines, or 3D models and distributing them standalone is treated as copyright infringement.

### D. Implying Official Endorsement
You must not use official company logos, publisher trademarks, or imply that your channel, product, or clips are sponsored or endorsed by the game's creators.

## 3. Official Publisher Video Policies & Direct Links

Every publisher has an official document governing video creation. You should **always read each publisher's policy yourself**:

- **Take-Two Interactive / Rockstar Games:**
  [Policy on Posting Copyrighted Material](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/200153756/Policy-on-posting-copyrighted-Rockstar-Games-material)
  *Key terms:* Permitted for non-commercial gameplay and walkthroughs; prohibits posting cutscenes without gameplay, complete game soundtracks, in-game radio music, and spoilers/leaks.
- **Valve Corporation:**
  [Valve Video Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/video_policy)
  *Key terms:* Encourages creating videos using Valve game content for non-commercial and platform-monetized content (YouTube/Twitch).
- **Blizzard Entertainment:**
  [Blizzard Video Policy](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/dd7642f3-363c-4452-b8ec-687004d7168d/blizzard-video-policy)
  *Key terms:* Free for community viewing; non-commercial production with standard platform advertising allowed; prohibits paywalled gameplay.
- **Electronic Arts (EA):**
  [EA YouTube & Video Policy / Terms of Service](https://www.ea.com/terms-of-service)
  *Key terms:* Permitted for personal, non-commercial gameplay videos with standard platform ad monetization.
- **Microsoft / Xbox Game Studios:**
  [Game Content Usage Rules](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/developers/rules)
  *Key terms:* Allows personal, non-commercial use of Microsoft game content to make videos and share them on platforms.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory Creator Responsibility:** Policies can change at any time. Game publishers and platforms set their own rules on game footage and monetization — check them before you post.
