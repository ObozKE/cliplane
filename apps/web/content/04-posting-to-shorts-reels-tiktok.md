# Posting to Shorts, Reels & TikTok: Platform Basics

Each short-form platform processes and re-encodes uploaded video files differently. Understanding platform-specific specifications ensures your clips look crisp and do not suffer from blurry compression or cropped UI elements.

---

## 1. Platform Comparison & Key Specs

| Spec | YouTube Shorts | TikTok | Instagram Reels |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 9:16 (1080x1920) | 9:16 (1080x1920) | 9:16 (1080x1920) |
| **Max Duration** | Up to 3 minutes (60s standard) | Up to 10 minutes | Up to 90 seconds |
| **File Format** | MP4, MOV (H.264 / AAC) | MP4, MOV (H.264) | MP4, MOV (H.264) |
| **Recommended Bitrate** | 10–16 Mbps | 8–12 Mbps | 8–12 Mbps |
| **Frame Rate** | 60 FPS supported | 60 FPS supported | 60 FPS (mobile app upload) |

## 2. Safe Margins (The "Safe Zone")

All three platforms overlay interactive UI elements (channel avatars, like buttons, comment icons, share arrows, audio titles, captions) on top of the vertical screen.

If your clip places important text or HUD elements along the bottom 20% or right-hand edge, they will be hidden by platform buttons:

- **Top 15%:** Keep free of essential text (channel search bar and live icons sit here).
- **Bottom 20%:** Keep clear of crucial gameplay info (caption text, hashtags, and sound title sit here).
- **Right 15%:** Keep clear of primary focus (action buttons: like, comment, bookmark, share).
- **Safe Center (70%):** The centered 16:9 gameplay rendered by ClipLane sits cleanly in the safest zone of the vertical canvas.

## 3. High-Quality Upload Settings

By default, some mobile apps compress uploads heavily over cellular data.

- **TikTok:** When publishing, tap **More options** → Turn ON **Allow high-quality uploads**.
- **Instagram:** Settings → **Account** → **Data usage** → Turn ON **Upload at highest quality**.
- **YouTube:** Uploading via desktop browser (YouTube Studio) often yields the most predictable 1080p60 H.264 transcoding without mobile bandwidth throttling.

## 4. Captions, Hashtags & Metadata

1. **Clear, Descriptive Titles:** Use 1–2 descriptive sentences highlighting the game and specific event (e.g., *"Clean sniper collateral in the final circle"* rather than *"omg look at this #viral #fyp"*).
2. **Relevant Game Tags:** Include the specific game name and subcategory (e.g. `#fps #sniper #gameplay`). Avoid 30 spam hashtags.
3. **Thumbnail Selection:** Choose a frame where the crosshair is centered or high action is evident. ClipLane automatically extracts a frame-accurate JPG thumbnail for every clip.
