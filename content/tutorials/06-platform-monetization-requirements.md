# What Platform Monetization Programs Require: Honest Criteria

The internet is full of deceptive claims promising that posting automatic gameplay clips will "make thousands overnight" or "guarantee passive income." Those claims are false.

Monetizing video content on major social platforms requires meeting strict, verifiable thresholds established by platform operators. Furthermore, all platforms maintain rigorous content quality reviews before admitting channels into revenue-sharing programs.

Here are the factual, up-to-date criteria for the major video networks.

---

## 1. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) — Shorts Criteria

To be accepted into the YouTube Partner Program and qualify for Shorts ad revenue sharing, a channel must meet either the standard long-form threshold **OR** the dedicated Shorts threshold:

### Eligibility Options
- **Shorts Pathway:** 
  - **1,000 subscribers**, AND
  - **10 million valid public Shorts views** within the last 90 days.
- **Alternative Pathway (Long-form):**
  - **1,000 subscribers**, AND
  - **4,000 valid public watch hours** on long-form videos within the last 12 months.

### The "Reused Content" Hurdle
This is the single most common reason gameplay clipping channels are rejected from YPP:
- YouTube's policy strictly penalizes **"Reused Content"**—defined as content that does not add significant original commentary or educational/creative value.
- Uploading pure, unedited clips of games without your voice, custom editing, skill demonstration, or narrative context frequently triggers automatic rejection during human channel review.
- **Best Practice:** Combine your gameplay highlights with commentary, thoughtful curation, skill analysis, or clear creator presence.

---

## 2. TikTok Creator Rewards Program

TikTok's primary revenue program has transitioned away from the old static Creator Fund to the **Creator Rewards Program**:

### Minimum Criteria
- Be at least 18 years old.
- Have at least **10,000 authentic followers**.
- Have accrued at least **100,000 valid video views** in the last 30 days.
- Maintain an account in good standing with zero active Community Guidelines violations.

### The 1-Minute+ Rule
- Under the Creator Rewards Program, only original videos **longer than 1 minute** are eligible for RPM rewards payouts.
- Standard short clips (under 60 seconds) are effective for audience discovery and follower growth, but do not directly qualify for Creator Rewards ad-pool distributions.

## 3. Instagram Reels & Subscriptions

Meta's creator monetization architecture differs by region:
- **Performance Bonuses:** Available in select markets by invitation only.
- **Creator Subscriptions & Gifts:** Typically require 10,000 followers and compliance with Meta's Partner Monetization Policies.
- **Branded Content:** Direct brand deals and sponsorships remain the most consistent monetization mechanism for gaming creators on Instagram.
